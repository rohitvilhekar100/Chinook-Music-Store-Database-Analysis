# Chinook Music Store Database Analysis

An end-to-end **SQL-based data analysis project** using the Chinook Music Store database to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product preferences, regional markets, customer churn, and customer risk using MySQL.

## 📁 Project Files

- **Chinook_Music_Store.sql** – SQL queries for objective and subjective business analysis
- **Chinook Music Store.docx** – Detailed analysis, approaches, observations, insights, and recommendations
- **Chinook Music Store.pptx** – Presentation containing key findings and visualizations

## 🔧 Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL / MySQL Workbench** – SQL analysis
- **SQL** – Data querying and business analysis
- **Microsoft Word / Google Docs** – Documentation
- **Microsoft PowerPoint / Google Slides** – Presentation and visualizations

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

- Customer distribution by country, state, and city
- Revenue and invoice analysis by geographic location
- Top-selling tracks and artists in the USA
- Genre-wise sales performance
- Top customers by revenue in each country
- Customer purchasing frequency and spending behavior
- Customer churn analysis
- Customers purchasing tracks from multiple genres
- Regional market and churn analysis
- Customer risk profiling
- Customer lifetime and purchasing analysis
- Promotional campaign impact analysis
- Average customer spending and tracks purchased by country

## 🧠 Key Insights

- **Rock** generated the highest USA genre revenue of **555.39**, contributing **53.38%** of USA genre sales.
- The customer base contains **59 customers across 24 countries**, with the USA having the highest number of customers.
- The calculated customer churn rate is **37.29%**, with 22 customers classified as churned under the defined three-month inactivity period.
- Customer purchasing behavior differs in terms of order frequency, average order value, total spending, and purchase history.
- Recent purchase activity is an important factor in identifying customers at higher risk.
- Sales, customer activity, and churn vary across different geographic regions.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the store can focus on:

- Re-engaging inactive and high-risk customers
- Using personalized recommendations based on customer purchase history
- Offering targeted promotions and loyalty offers
- Monitoring customer churn regularly
- Focusing marketing efforts on high-performing genres and products
- Using regional sales and churn patterns to support marketing decisions

## 🚀 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate practical **SQL, data analysis, and business problem-solving skills** by converting data from a relational music store database into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

## 📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL techniques such as **JOINs, GROUP BY, aggregations, CTEs, subqueries, and window functions** to solve real-world business analysis questions using the Chinook Music Store database.
